# Contributing to Vibe Craft Studios projects

Thanks for helping! Bug reports and ideas are welcome through the issue forms of each repository.

- **Code** you contribute is licensed under the license of the repository (Apache-2.0 for most of them, see
  [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). Only submit code you wrote yourself or may share under that license.
- **Artwork, models, textures and sounds:** by contributing them you confirm that you made them yourself (or may
  share them) and agree that Vibe Craft Studios may use, change and distribute them as part of its projects. They
  fall under [ASSETS-POLICY.md](ASSETS-POLICY.md), with credit to you.
- Keep pull requests focused, describe what you changed and how you tested it.
- Never commit secrets (tokens, keys, `.env` files).

Forking for your own project? Please read [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) and [SERVICES.md](SERVICES.md) first.

# Security

Please report security problems **privately**: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Don't open a public issue for them.

This covers our apps and mods as well as the Vibe online services (vibe.axolite.app, formerly vibe.henny.dev, and the Vibey relay at *.axolite.cc). Please don't
test against other people's accounts or try to break the services' availability.
